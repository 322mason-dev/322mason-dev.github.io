About Me

I’m a developer and designer focused on building work that feels clear, intentional, and visually strong.

Hi, I’m Mason.

I’m a Digital Design and Computing Technology student at Arcadia University, with a minor in Graphic Design. I focus on web development and graphic design, creating work that blends clean design with personality and character.

I’m especially interested in where design and development overlap. I like building websites that not only look good, but feel smooth to use, make sense to navigate, and actually do what they’re supposed to do.

Through both school and freelance work, I’ve developed a process centered around structure, usability, and strong visual identity. Whether I’m designing a brand or building a full website, I’m always aiming to make something cohesive, intentional, and worth using.

Background

My background is in front-end development, branding, and visual design. Between school, freelance work, and personal projects, I’ve spent a lot of time building things and refining how I approach both the technical and visual sides of a project.

Experience

I’ve worked on a mix of academic projects, portfolio pieces, and real client work, with a focus on building responsive websites and clean, cohesive designs. I’m always looking to keep improving and push both my design and development skills further.

Approach

I like work that feels intentional. Design should communicate clearly, and development should support that instead of getting in the way. I focus on making things that feel clean, easy to use, and actually thought through.
